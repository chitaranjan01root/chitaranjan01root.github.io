Hazari 1000 Scoreboard

The companion card scoreboard app for Hazari 1000 players. Keep score easily, hear voice announcements, and view match statistics.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Hazari 1000 Scoreboard does not collect, store, or sell any personal data. All match history and player names are saved locally on your device.

Third-Party Services & Ads

The app uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and use device identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID) and non-personal diagnostic data to serve relevant ads according to Google's Privacy Policy at policies.google.com/privacy.
