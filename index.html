Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Hazari & Callbreak mobile application (Package Name: com.havis.hazari), operated by Havis ("we", "us", or "our").

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: If you choose to sign in using Google Sign-In, we collect your public display name, email address, and avatar reference via Firebase Authentication to create and synchronize your player profile across devices.
  • Game Statistics: Match scores, win rates, leaderboard rankings, and in-game achievements are stored in Cloud Firestore to enable global and national leaderboards.
  • Device Identifiers & Advertising Data: We use Google AdMob, which may collect anonymous device identifiers, advertising IDs, and app interaction data to serve personalized or non-personalized advertisements.

2. Device Permissions Used

Camera Permission (android.permission.CAMERA):
The camera is used strictly on-device to scan Table QR Codes for peer-to-peer Hotspot multiplayer lobbies. We do NOT record, capture, store, or transmit any photos, videos, or camera data to any external server.
  • Nearby Devices / Local WiFi: Used solely to discover and connect players on the same local network/hotspot for offline multiplayer games. No location tracking is performed.

3. Third-Party Services

Our application integrates trusted third-party services that have their own privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services & Google Sign-In
  • Google Firebase (Auth & Firestore)
  • Google AdMob

4. Data Security

All network communications (leaderboards, authentication, ads) are transmitted securely using encrypted TLS/HTTPS connections. Sensitive preferences and tokens are excluded from unencrypted device backups.

5. Data Deletion & User Rights

You can sign out of your account at any time within the Profile settings. If you wish to delete your synced leaderboard records or user data, you can request full account deletion by contacting us at the email below.

6. Children’s Privacy

Our game does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us for immediate removal.
